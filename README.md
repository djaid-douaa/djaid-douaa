I'm an AI engineer from ENSIA (National Higher School of AI, Algiers). Most of what I build sits around large language models: agents that plan and call tools, retrieval pipelines that keep answers grounded in real documents, and the plumbing that turns a notebook into something people can actually use.

Lately that has meant routing, MCP connectors and approval guardrails for an agentic desktop OS at BACR, and a multi-agent system that writes cardiology reports in French for my engineering thesis.

**Open to:** AI / ML engineer roles (remote or relocation) · funded PhD positions in agentic and trustworthy LLM systems · research collaborations
<br>**Languages:** Arabic · French · English
